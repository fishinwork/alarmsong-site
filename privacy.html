Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026. AlarmSong is run by an individual developer. Write to hello@alarmsong.com about anything on this page.

AlarmSong writes a song about your day. To do that it has to read what your day looks like, and send it to services that write the words and make the music. This page says what leaves your phone, why, and how to make it stop. We have tried to write it plainly, because a privacy policy nobody can read protects nobody.

What we collect, and why

  • Your day. The titles and start times of your calendar events for the day of the alarm, and a few titles from the day before — only if you allow calendar access. Nothing else is read from an event. This is what the song is about.
  • What you tell the app. The name you want to be called and how to refer to you, the music styles, voice and language you picked, and anything you typed yourself. The name does not have to be your real one.
  • Your alarm. The date, your wake-up time and your time zone, so the song is ready in time and knows what day it is.
  • A device identifier supplied by your phone’s operating system. It keeps your songs attached to your phone while you have no account, lets you delete them, and helps us apply fair-use limits and protect the service from misuse. It is not your advertising ID and it does not follow you between apps.
  • Your network address, which every internet request carries. We use it to protect the service, to apply fair-use limits, and to estimate which city you are in so the song can mention the weather — to about ten kilometres, never to a street. The city is not stored, and the app never asks your phone for your location.
  • Account details, only if you create an account: your email address, the name your sign-in provider gives us, and which provider it was. An account exists so your songs survive a new phone. You can use AlarmSong without one.
  • Reports. If you report a song, we keep what you wrote and which song it was about, so that a person can look at it.

What we do not do

We do not read your contacts, messages, photos or browsing. We do not track you across apps or sites. We show no advertising, and we do not sell or rent anything about you to anyone. There are no advertising or tracking tools inside the app.

This website counts visits without cookies, using an analytics service that stores nothing in your browser and cannot recognise you on a second visit. Nothing from the app reaches it.

Your calendar is not stored. Event titles travel to our server, become the words of a song, and are not written to any database of ours. What remains afterwards is the song itself, which contains those words because it sings about them.

Who else sees your day

We do not make the song alone. What is needed for it is passed to service providers who work for us:

  • Third-party artificial-intelligence services that write the lyrics and create the music. They receive the content of your request — your event titles, the name you chose, your music taste and anything you typed — and the finished lyrics.
  • A weather service, which receives approximate coordinates and the date, and nothing else.
  • Infrastructure providers: the hosting where your songs are stored, and the email service that delivers sign-in codes.
  • Apple and Google, if you sign in with them or buy something. They tell us that a person signed in; they do not get your calendar.

These providers act for us under their own terms, and we require them to protect what we send with safeguards comparable to our own. We do not give them permission to sell it or to use it for their own advertising. They are located in different countries, including the United States, Europe and Asia, so your data may be processed outside the country you live in.

How long we keep things

  • Songs without an account: three days, then the file and its words are deleted automatically.
  • Songs with an account: thirty days. Songs you marked with a heart are kept until you remove the heart or delete your account.
  • The copy on your phone: one week. The app keeps its own copy of each song so you can play it without the internet, and clears it after seven days. Songs you hearted stay. Deleting the app removes all of it from your phone; if your phone’s own backup includes app data, a copy can remain there, under your control.
  • Reports: up to thirty days.
  • Your account: until you delete it.
  • Network addresses and sign-in codes: a short time, normally no more than a day or two. Server logs: up to thirty days.

After deletion we keep only records that do not identify you and that we need for our own accounting, and — if a device or an account was restricted for misuse — the minimum needed to keep that restriction in place.

Your choices

Refuse the calendar, or take it back. The app asks before it reads anything. You can withdraw that permission at any time in your phone’s settings, and the app keeps working without it.

Delete everything. With an account: Settings → Account → Delete account. Without one: Settings → Delete my songs. Both are immediate and final. If you no longer have the app, write to hello@alarmsong.com from the address you signed in with. The full description is on the deletion page.

Ask us what we hold. Write to the same address and we will send you a copy, or correct something that is wrong. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, these are your rights under the GDPR — access, correction, erasure, portability, objection — and you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

The legal basis for making your song is the agreement between us; for reading your calendar it is your explicit consent, which you can withdraw whenever you like.

Children

AlarmSong is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. If you believe a child has used it, write to us and we will erase what we have.

Changes

If we make a significant change, we will update this page and the date at the top.
