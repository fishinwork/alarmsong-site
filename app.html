What you are getting

An alarm clock that behaves like one — the real system alarm, ringing through a locked screen and a closed app — and a song written that night about the day in front of you. The meeting at ten is in the chorus.

A hundred music styles or your own words. Songs kept for a week, or for good if you heart them. No maths to solve at seven in the morning.

What it needs

  • An iPhone with iOS 26

    That is where an iPhone lets an app ring its own alarm rather than a notification, and the whole point is that it rings like an alarm.

    The beta does install from iOS 18, and on those phones it still writes your song and plays it — it just cannot wake you. We would rather say that now than have you find out at seven in the morning.

  • A connection in the evening

    The song is written the night before and kept on the phone. In the morning it plays with no connection at all.

  • A calendar, ideally

    Not required. Without one the song is about the morning rather than your day — it works, it is just less pointed.
