What the app does

AlarmSong is an alarm clock. Every morning it wakes you with a song written for that day. The songs are written and performed by artificial intelligence, from what your day looks like: the events on your calendar, the style of music you picked, the name you gave us. No human writes or sings them.

That means a song can be odd, wrong about your day, or simply bad. It is a machine making something new every morning, not a person checking the result. If a song ever says something it should not have, tell us and we will look at it.
